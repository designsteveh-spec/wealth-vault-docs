# Financial Disclosure — Wealth Vault for Android

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

This note is for the **Android** app. The Windows app has its [own disclosure](../financial-disclosure.html).

Wealth Vault is **personal finance organization software**. It is not a broker, advisor, bank, or tax preparer.

## No advisory relationship

Using the app does not create a financial, brokerage, legal, or tax-preparation relationship between you and Wealth Vault or its creators.

## What the numbers are

Totals, charts, and leftover-cash figures are **informational**. They are built from:

- Amounts you type
- Files you import
- Optional quotes, only if a later version offers them and you turn them on

A chart is not a forecast. Past totals do not guarantee future results. Markets, tax rules, and your own accounts change.

The app does not log in to your bank. A file you import can be incomplete, especially because deposits are left out. Check important figures against your own statements.

## No guarantee

Wealth Vault is provided without a warranty that it will meet a financial goal, prevent a loss, or produce a calculation with no errors.
