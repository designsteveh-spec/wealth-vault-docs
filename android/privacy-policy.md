# Privacy Policy — Wealth Vault for Android

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

This policy is for the **Android** app. The Windows app on the Microsoft Store has its [own privacy policy](../privacy-policy.html).

## Summary

Wealth Vault on Android is a companion for looking at your figures and logging spending. Your records stay **private on the phone**. We do not run a cloud ledger of your portfolio, budget, or daily spend, and the app does not ask you to log in to a bank.

## What stays on your phone

The app stores on the device:

- Project names
- Purchases, holdings, debts, and notes you enter
- Bank and brokerage files you choose to import (CSV, TXT, Excel, OFX, or QFX), read on the phone. That includes a bank download and a brokerage file such as a Vanguard activity report or a Schwab positions download.
- Charts and totals built from those records
- Settings, such as light or dark appearance

A file you import is not uploaded. Deposits in a bank download are skipped. The app does not connect to your bank and does not ask for bank login credentials.

## What we do not do

Wealth Vault does not:

- Require an account or a bank login
- Sell your personal information
- Show ads
- Use your files to train AI models

Do not type passwords, full account numbers, Social Security numbers, or wallet seed phrases into the app. A bank download may already contain a masked account label from your bank. That label stays on the phone with the rest of the file.

## Purchases

**Free**, **Vault Lifetime**, and **Vault Plus** are billed by **Google Play** when you buy them. Google processes the payment. Wealth Vault does not receive your card number. Google may keep purchase records under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Optional later features

The first version does not send your vault anywhere.

If a later **Vault Plus** feature offers live quotes, a quote request may include a ticker symbol. It does not include your balances or purchases.

If a later feature lets you move a vault file between this phone and the Windows app, that file moves only when you choose to send it. The first version does not keep a copy of your vault on a Wealth Vault server.

## Google Play

If you install from Google Play, Google may collect install, crash, and diagnostics data under Google's own policies. That collection is separate from the records Wealth Vault keeps on your phone.

## Exports and deletion

You can export spending and holdings as CSV. Those files go where you save them.

Uninstalling the app removes the records stored in the app on that phone. We do not keep a server copy to delete, because we do not hold one. Files you already exported stay wherever you saved them.

## Children's privacy

Wealth Vault is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top will change when we do.
