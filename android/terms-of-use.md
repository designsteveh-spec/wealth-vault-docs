## About the app

**Wealth Vault** on Android is a companion to the Windows app. It lets you see a summary, log spending, review holdings and debts you entered, and import a bank file you downloaded yourself. Spreadsheet mapping and the long planning worksheets stay on the computer.

Your projects stay on the phone unless you export them.

## Plans

Google Play handles payment.

- **Free** — one project, logging and editing purchases by hand, and a short history
- **Vault Lifetime** — a one-time purchase. Import and export, the fuller charts, a longer history, and up to three projects. You keep that version if you never subscribe
- **Vault Plus** — a subscription, billed yearly or monthly. Unlimited projects, and later features such as live quotes, moving a vault file to the Windows app, and charts added during a paid year

Cancel Plus and a Lifetime purchase you already bought stays. A code bought on a website cannot unlock the Android app.

Prices shown in the app are the prices charged on Google Play.
