# Privacy Policy — Wealth Vault

*Last updated: August 2026*

## Summary

Wealth Vault is a **local-first** desktop personal finance app. Your detailed financial records stay on your Windows computer under your user profile. We do not operate a cloud ledger of your portfolio, budget, or daily spend data.

## What stays on your device

By default, Wealth Vault stores on your PC:

- Project names and settings
- Imported spreadsheet files
- Manual edits, budget lines, liabilities, tax-deduction notes, and planning inputs
- Encrypted local databases and UI state

Nothing in the above is uploaded to Wealth Vault servers for storage or analytics, because we do not operate such servers for your project data.

## Optional network use

When you choose **Limited online** connectivity, the app may request **market quotes** and similar public market data for tickers you use (for example via third-party quote providers). Those requests send **ticker symbols** (and similar identifiers needed for quotes), not your full project file or net-worth breakdown.

Wealth Vault does **not**:

- Require bank login or Plaid-style account linking
- Sell your personal data
- Use your files to train AI models

When you choose **Offline mode**, the app stops making new quote requests and keeps working locally.

## Microsoft Store

If you install from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect standard Store analytics and crash data under [Microsoft's privacy policies](https://privacy.microsoft.com/). That collection is separate from Wealth Vault's local data model.

## Updates

The app may check for **app updates** through the Microsoft Store. Release notes are published in this repository.

## Children's privacy

Wealth Vault is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or distribution channel changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
