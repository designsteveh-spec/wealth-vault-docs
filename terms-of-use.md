## About the software

**Wealth Vault** is a personal finance workspace that runs on your Windows computer. It helps you organize projects built from spreadsheets, manual entries, and in-app views so you can see holdings, liabilities, budgets, notated deductions, summaries, and simple retirement and planning scenarios in one place.

Wealth Vault is **free** on the Microsoft Store with full access to every vault page and offline mode. Your projects and files stay on your device unless you export them.
